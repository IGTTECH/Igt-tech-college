This Privacy Policy describes how IGT TECH COLLEGE ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information in connection with your use of the mobile application Chuo cha Simu (the "Application").

We are committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws worldwide.

🔒 Summary of Core Principles:
• Offline-First Architecture: Educational content, quizzes, and certificate generation run locally on your device.
• No Covert Tracking: We do NOT collect, transmit, or sell your personal identifying information (PII), biometric data, location data, or contact lists.
• Zero-Permission Media Access: We use modern Android Photo Picker without requesting broad storage permissions.

1. Information We Collect and Process

The Application operates primarily as an on-device local utility. The following categories of data are processed:

Data Category Type of Data Storage Location & Purpose
Student Profile Name Optional text string entered by user Stored locally in SQLite/Room Database on the device. Used exclusively to imprint your name onto the Certificate of Completion. Never transmitted to external servers.
Exam & Course Progress Scores, completed lesson IDs, timestamps Stored locally on-device in isolated app storage. Used to calculate course completion percentages and certificate eligibility.
Profile Photo / Avatar Selected image file via Android Photo Picker Cached locally in private application storage solely for display on your certificate card. We do not transmit or scan images.
Technical Diagnostic Data Device model, OS version, crash logs Standard aggregated diagnostic data processed by Google Play Console and Appodeal SDK for performance and crash reporting.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Appodeal SDK)

To provide free educational vocational courses to students worldwide, the free tier of the Application displays advertisements served by Appodeal SDK and its verified mediation network partners:

  • AppLovin (Privacy Policy)
  • Meta Audience Network / Facebook (Privacy Policy)
  • Unity Ads (Privacy Policy)
  • BidMachine (Privacy Policy)

These advertising partners may process non-sensitive device identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and network provider to deliver contextual and relevant advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure campaign effectiveness, in compliance with Google Play Advertising Policies.

Opting Out / Resetting Advertising ID: Users can reset or delete their Advertising ID at any time via Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete advertising ID. Users may also purchase the PRO (Ad-Free) upgrade to disable advertising entirely.

3. In-App Purchases & Financial Data (Google Play Billing)

All purchases (including the PRO Ad-Free license) are executed exclusively through the official Google Play In-App Billing API.

We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, bank account numbers, or billing addresses. All payment transactions are securely handled by Google LLC under the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Device Permissions Explained

In strict compliance with Google Play's Least Privilege Permission Principle, the Application requests only permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for loading educational web references, verifying Google Play purchase status, and serving mediation ads in the free tier.
  • Photo Selection (Android Photo Picker): When you choose a photo for your Certificate of Completion, the system utilizes the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). We do NOT request or require broad storage permissions such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.
  • PDF Export: Downloaded certificates are written using standard Android MediaStore / scoped storage APIs directly to your public Downloads/Documents directory with no intrusive file system scanning.

5. Data Retention and Deletion (User Control)

Because all educational and profile records are kept locally on your hardware, you retain 100% control over your data:

  • In-App Reset: You can edit or erase your student name and retake examination quizzes at any time from within the app.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can instantly purge all local application data, scores, and saved certificates by navigating to: Android Settings > Apps > Chuo cha Simu > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

The Application is a general-audience technical education app aimed at teenagers and adults interested in smartphone repair, electronics, and cybersecurity. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

7. Application Security & Integrity

We implement industry-standard cryptographic techniques, Android sandboxing, ProGuard/R8 code minification, and anti-tamper safeguards to protect the Application against unauthorized modification, reverse engineering, and injection of malicious code.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements, security practices, or app functionality. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
