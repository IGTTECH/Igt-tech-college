Why Was IGT Camera Tools Created?

In today’s fast-moving world, professionals across civil engineering, environmental research, construction, insurance verification, logistics, security, and content creation struggle with fragmented software. Standard smartphone camera apps strip out vital GPS metadata during messaging transfers, single-purpose video compressors degrade video quality or upload sensitive files to remote third-party servers, and basic photo editors lack real-time geolocation capabilities.

IGT Camera Tools solves this challenge by unifying the entire mobile media production pipeline into a single, cohesive, ultra-fast application. Built from the ground up using modern Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and native Android MediaCodec & OpenGL ES 2.0 graphics pipelines, the application delivers desktop-grade media manipulation directly into the palm of your hand — with total offline reliability, zero subscription lockouts, and uncompromising data privacy.

Proprietary Technology

Hardware-Accelerated Video Geo-Overlay Burner

Standard camera apps store GPS metadata in hidden file EXIF headers that get permanently wiped the instant you share a video over WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, or email. IGT Camera Tools permanently solves this issue.

Using our custom VideoGeoOverlayBurner hardware pipeline (powered by Android MediaCodec, EGL14, and OpenGL ES 2.0 shaders), the app renders an obsidian glass card containing live GIS map previews, sub-meter GPS coordinates, GMT timestamps, location hierarchy (village, district, region, country), and real-time environmental sensors directly into the video frames during encoding.

  • Unalterable Verification: Permanent on-screen evidence that cannot be forged or stripped by messaging apps.
  • Rotation Safety: Intelligently adapts layout boundaries in landscape mode so the overlay never dominates or obstructs the video frame.
  • Zero Audio Sync Drift: Direct pass-through audio multiplexing prevents sound desynchronization on long field recordings.
