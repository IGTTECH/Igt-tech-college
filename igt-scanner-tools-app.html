Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about IGT Scanner Tools? We have answers.

Is IGT Scanner Tools free to download and use?
Yes! You can download and start scanning, generating codes, and digitizing documents immediately on Android without any upfront cost.
Does the app require an active internet connection to scan codes?
No! All barcode decoding, QR parsing, document camera filtering, PDF generation, and QR creation happen 100% locally on your device. An internet connection is only needed if you wish to open external web links or search web results.
What barcode and QR formats are supported?
The app supports all standard industry formats: QR Code, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, UPC-A, UPC-E, EAN-13, EAN-8, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, and Codabar.
Can I scan codes from screenshots or saved gallery photos?
Yes. Tap the Gallery icon on the scanner screen, select any saved image or screenshot, and the app will instantly detect and decode any barcode or QR code present in the photo.
How does the Document Cam Scanner to PDF feature work?
Simply take photos of your physical documents (invoices, receipts, contracts, notes). The app lets you arrange multi-page documents, apply black-and-white contrast enhancement filters, and export a clean, high-resolution PDF ready for emailing or printing.
How does History PIN Security protect my confidential data?
In the app settings, you can enable PIN Protection and set a 4-digit security code. Once activated, anyone accessing your scan history will be required to enter the PIN, preventing unauthorized access if you lend your phone.
