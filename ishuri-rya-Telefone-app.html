Ibyo Uzasanga Muri Iyi Aplikasiyo

Gukora Hardware ya Telefone

Amasomo arambuye ku gusanura no gusimbura za Ekran (LCD/OLED), Amasohokero ya Bateri (Charging Ports), Bateri, Speakers, na Micro-Soldering ku Bwubako Bwa Motherboard.

Software & Operating Systems

Kwiga gukora Flashing, Unlocking, Kukuraho FRP Lock, Gukora System Recovery, n'Ibyerekeye icyitwa Firmware update mu buryo bwizewe kandi butagira ingaruka kuri telefone.

Cyber Security & Umutekano

Uburyo bwo kurinda telefone n'amakuru yawe kwinjirirwa (Anti-Hacking), Kurinda WhatsApp, Social Media, Kumenya Phishing Links, no Gucunga Passwords neza.

Troubleshooting & FAQs Engine
